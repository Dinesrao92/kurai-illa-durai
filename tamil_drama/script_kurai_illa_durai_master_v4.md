# 🎬 KURAI ILLA, DURAI
### Master Screenplay — Part 1
**Status:** Scene 1 FINAL / Story direction LOCKED
**Setting:** Brickfields, Kuala Lumpur
**Language:** Malaysian Tamil / Thanglish

---

## STORY CANON — LOCKED

### DURAI
- Late 50s. Old-school, respected gangster figure in Brickfields.
- Publicly operates multiple legitimate businesses around Brickfields.
- His most visible day-to-day presence is the flower and garland trade.
- Durai does **not** sell drugs, smuggle illegal goods, exploit innocent people, or kill for business.
- He protects the people and neighbourhood around him, but handles threats using the methods and reputation of a gangster.
- His power comes from relationships, loyalty, influence and fear of crossing his code.
- His central moral contrast with Maaran: **Durai is a gangster by reputation and method, but a protector by principle.**

### KAVIN
- Late 20s.
- An orphan whom Durai found on the streets **10 years ago** and raised.
- Durai is effectively Kavin's father figure.
- Kavin is Durai's trusted left hand.
- Kavin specifically handles the **garland/flower business**; he does not run all of Durai's businesses.
- He appears harmless, casual and inexperienced in fighting.
- Unknown to everyone, Kavin spends **six months undercover inside Maaran's organisation**.
- His undercover mission is to expose and trap Maaran's criminal network.
- The audience should not know about the six-month undercover operation until the climax.

### MAARAN
- The apparent main villain of Part 1.
- A powerful crime boss with a large gang network.
- Involved in drugs, smuggling, murder and other serious criminal activity.
- Uses fear, money and violence to control people.
- He believes Durai is an outdated gangster whose influence can be taken over.
- **Important Part 1 twist:** Maaran is not the true top of the criminal organisation.

### THE MYSTERIOUS MAN
- Maaran's real superior.
- His identity is **not revealed in Part 1**.
- The audience discovers only at the climax that Maaran answers to someone above him.
- This character is the primary bridge into Part 2.

# SCENE 1 — BRICKFIELDS BEFORE DAWN

### EXT. BRICKFIELDS — 5:15 A.M.

Before the city wakes up.

The streets are still wet from last night's rain.

A lorry unloads flower baskets.

Jasmine.

Roses.

Chrysanthemums.

Marigolds.

The smell of fresh flowers mixes with the smell of wet road, diesel and the first few cups of teh tarik being poured at a nearby mamak.

A pair of old hands sorts jasmine flowers into a large basket.

We don't see the man's face.

Only his hands.

**FLOWER SELLER**
Durai anna... indha order ready pannala?

**DURAI (O.S.)**
Innum rendu nimisham.

He finishes.

Cuts the thread with his teeth.

Only then do we see him.

**DURAI — late 50s.**

Simple white shirt.

Veshti.

Old sandals.

No gold chains.

No sunglasses.

No flashy car.

Just a man sitting among flowers at five in the morning.

He lifts the garland.

Checks it.

Satisfied.

Hands it over.

**DURAI**
Po. Kovil-ku late aagidum.

The seller takes it and leaves.

Durai continues working.

A young boy approaches.

**BOY**
Anna... amma sonnaanga, innikku kaasu naalaikku kudukkalaama?

Durai looks at him.

**DURAI**
Evlo?

**BOY**
Thirty.

Durai takes thirty ringgit from his pocket.

Hands it to him.

**DURAI**
Poi kuduthudu.

The boy smiles.

Runs away.

Durai goes back to his flowers.

A beat.

Then—

A MOTORCYCLE stops nearby.

Two unfamiliar men get off.

They look around.

One of them notices Durai.

They whisper.

Durai doesn't look at them.

But he knows they're there.

One man walks toward him.

**MAN**
Durai?

Durai keeps tying the garland.

**DURAI**
Sollu.

**MAN**
Maaran anna solli anuppichaaru.

Durai finally looks up.

The flower shop suddenly feels quieter.

**DURAI**
Enna solli?

The man smiles.

**MAN**
Brickfields-la konjam business expand panna poraaru.

Durai nods slowly.

Looks around at the street.

The shops.

The flower sellers.

The temple workers.

The people beginning their morning.

Then looks back at the man.

**DURAI**
Business-aa?

**MAN**
Aama.

Durai picks up another garland.

Continues working.

**DURAI**
Business pannunga.

The man smiles.

**MAN**
Appo problem illa?

Durai looks at him.

A calm smile.

**DURAI**
Business-ku problem illa.

Beat.

**DURAI**
Aana... unga business enna-nu therinjikanum.

The man's smile disappears.

Durai's eyes move toward a small plastic bag hanging from the motorcycle.

He doesn't need to open it.

He already knows.

**DURAI**
Adha inga kondu vara koodadhu.

Silence.

**MAN**
Maaran anna sonna—

Durai interrupts.

**DURAI**
Naan Maaran illa.

A beat.

**DURAI**
Idhu Brickfields.

The two men exchange a look.

Durai goes back to his flowers.

As though the conversation is finished.

The men leave.

The motorcycle disappears into the morning traffic.

Durai continues making garlands.

### CUT TO:

### EXT. BRICKFIELDS — MORNING

The neighbourhood is alive now.

Durai walks through the streets carrying a basket of flowers.

People greet him.

**AUNTY**
Durai! Kaalaila vandhuttiya?

**DURAI**
Vandhuten.

A shopkeeper waves.

A temple worker takes a garland.

A fruit seller asks about his son.

Durai knows everyone's name.

Everyone knows his.

From across the street, we see another figure watching him.

A young man carrying several flower baskets.

**KAVIN — late 20s.**

He looks ordinary.

Friendly.

A little mischievous.

He shouts:

**KAVIN**
Durai anna!

Durai turns.

Kavin holds up a garland.

**KAVIN**
Idhu enga poganum?

Durai points down the street.

**DURAI**
Muthu kadai.

Kavin looks at the enormous pile of garlands.

**KAVIN**
Muthu kadai-aa?

Beat.

**KAVIN**
Naan oru aal dhaane anna.

Durai smiles.

**DURAI**
Adhaan unna valarthadhu.

Kavin laughs.

He walks away carrying the flowers.

Durai watches him for a moment.

There is affection in his eyes.

Not a boss looking at his employee.

Something deeper.

## CLIMAX CANON — LOCKED

There is **no police presence in the climax**.

Durai and Maaran have a heated verbal confrontation.

Maaran attacks Durai.

Kavin enters and unexpectedly reveals that he can fight.

The audience and characters have believed Kavin is harmless and cannot fight.

Kavin overwhelms Maaran and badly injures him.

The reveal follows: Kavin has secretly been undercover inside Maaran's organisation for **six months**.

Maaran is not the ultimate villain.

While badly injured, Maaran makes a phone call to a mysterious man who is actually the much bigger head of the crime organisation.

**Part 1 ends without revealing the mysterious man's identity.**

---

## CORE THEME

**Maaran uses fear to control people.  
Durai uses fear to protect them.**

Durai's goodness must be shown through his actions rather than repeatedly explained by other characters.

Kavin's climax reveal is also the payoff to the **10-year bond between Durai and Kavin**: the orphan Durai found on the streets has grown into the man Durai trusted to protect him and the people around them.
