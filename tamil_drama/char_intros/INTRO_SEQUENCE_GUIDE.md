## SCENE 02 — Getting your 10–15 sec

**Options to hit the length on free tiers:**
- **Kling web (10s max free):** generate 10s + in CapCut hold the final frame for 1.5s (Freeze function) while audio finishes

- **Kling VIDEO 3.0 app (if you buy/have credits):** 15s single take with speech

**Editing tip for intro cards:** add a quick "whoosh + bass drop" SFX (CapCut library) exactly when each name appears — DURAI 💥, KAVIN 💥, MAARAN 💥. Instant mass feel.
