# 🎬 KURAI ILLA, DURAI — PART 2: "AARAMBAM"
### (The Beginning — Flashback Episode)
**Structure:** Opens in the PRESENT (right after Part 1's ending), then flashes back **6 months earlier** to reveal how everything was set up — and ends with a hook for Part 3.

**Characters (same cast):**
- **DURAI ANNA** (voice-00) — also the flashback NARRATOR
- **KAVIN** (voice-01)
- **MAARAN** (voice-02)

**Flashback visual tip:** give flashback scenes a slightly warmer/faded filter in CapCut so viewers instantly know it's the past. Add a "6 MAASAM MUNNADI" (6 months earlier) text card at the transition.

---

## SCENE 1 — PRESENT DAY (night, mamak stall, after Part 1)

*(SFX: light rain, teh tarik pouring, distant traffic)*

**1. DURAI:** *(narration tone, reflective)*
> Ellarum ennakitta ketkuranga... "Anna, Kavin-a epdi ivlo nambureenga?"-nu. Hmm... indha kadhai innaikku aarambam aanadhu illa da. Idhu ellam aarambam aanadhu... aaru maasam munnadi.

*(SFX: whoosh — FLASHBACK TRANSITION — "6 MAASAM MUNNADI" title card)*

## SCENE 2 — FLASHBACK: Mamak stall, late night

*(Maaran slides into the seat opposite Kavin)*

**2. MAARAN:** *(smooth, recruiting)*
> Dey thambi... Kavin dhaana? Un pathi nalla kelvi patten da. Durai kitta velai seiyara... aana sambalam evlo? Kanjipono. Kelu — en kooda vandha, oru maasam-la nee Myvi illa... BMW oottuva da!

**3. KAVIN:** *(uncomfortable, careful)*
> Yaru nee? Enna da solra? Durai anna enakku appa maadhiri da. Avara vittutu naan... che! Indha maadhiri pechu pesina nalla irukkadhu. Poidu da inganendhu!

**4. MAARAN:** *(standing up, dropping a name card)*
> Aiyo, kovama? Paravala... yosichu paaru. Durai kaalam mudinjidhu da. Kappal mungumbodhu eli dhaan modhala kudhikkum. Nee smart eli-nu nenakkiren. En number vachiko... call pannuva. Naan wait panren, thambi.

## SCENE 3 — FLASHBACK: Durai's office, next morning

*(Kavin bursts in, agitated)*

**5. KAVIN:** *(urgent, loyal)*
> Anne! Nethu raathiri oruthan vandhan anne — Maaran-nu. Ungala vittutu avan pakkam vara sonnan. Kaasu, car, ellam promise pannan anne. Naan appove ungakitta solanum-nu vandhen!

**6. DURAI:** *(calm, already knowing — lights cigarette)*
> Maaran... Johor-lendhu vandha pudhu payyan. Enakku munnadiye theriyum da avan en area mela kannu vachirukkan-nu. Aana nee... nee nerra vandhu sonniye — adhan da unmaiyana aalu. Ippo kelu... enakku oru plan irukku.

**7. DURAI:** *(the plan — leaning in, low voice)*
> Nee avan kooda seru. Avan nambikkaiya sambadhichuko. Avan enna pannuraan, yaaru kooda pesuraan — ellam enakku theriyanum. Aana jaakirudha da thambi... avan munnadi nee ENNODA edhiri maadhiri nadikkanum. Mudiyuma unnala?

**8. KAVIN:** *(scared but determined)*
> Anne... bayama dhaan irukku anne. Aana neenga sonna, naan seiven. Oru condition — en thangachi kalyanathukku neenga dhaan mudhal aal ah vandhu aasirvadham pannanum!

*(Both laugh — bond moment)*

## SCENE 4 — FLASHBACK MONTAGE: 6 months undercover

*(Fast cuts: Kavin with Maaran's gang, secret phone calls, lorry plans)*

**9. MAARAN:** *(bragging to his gang, Kavin beside him)*
> Paarunga da! Indha Kavin — Durai-oda chellam — ippo NAMMA aalu! Adutha vaaram Durai-oda lorry varudhu. Andha saman naama adichoma... Durai mudinja kadhai! KL theru pora pokkula... Maaran theru aagum da!

**10. DURAI:** *(narration over montage — the reveal)*
> Aana avanukku theriyadhu... andha lorry-la saman ille, en plan dhaan irundhadhu-nu. Kavin anuppuna ovvoru message-um... neraa police kitta poachu. Aaru maasam. Ovvoru adi, ovvoru kaasu, ovvoru kolai pechu — ellam record aaguchu. Appo dhaan... andha raathiri vandhadhu. Neenga Part 1-la paatha andha raathiri.

*(SFX: whoosh — BACK TO PRESENT)*

## SCENE 5 — PRESENT DAY: The hook 🪝

*(Durai sips his teh tarik... then his face slowly darkens)*

**11. DURAI:** *(final line — new mystery)*
> Aana... innum oru kelvi en manasula kuthudhu da. Maaran-ukku en lorry route... DATE... TIME... ellam correct-a epdi therinjadhu? Kavin sollala. Appo yaaru?... En veettukulla... INNORU paambu irukku da. *(camera slow zoom on Durai's eyes)* Part 3-la paapom.

*(SFX: thunder crack — CUT TO BLACK — title: "KURAI ILLA, DURAI — PART 3: PAAMBU" coming soon)*
