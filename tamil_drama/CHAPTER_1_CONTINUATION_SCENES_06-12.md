# SCENE 6 — THE PRICE OF FEAR

### EXT. BRICKFIELDS FLOWER MARKET — MORNING

The market is busy.

But something is missing.

The usual laughter between vendors.

The usual bargaining.

Everyone keeps glancing toward the road.

The three MEN from Maaran's side stand beside a motorcycle.

Nobody pays them.

One vendor quietly closes his cash box.

The men notice.

**MAN #1**
Enna da idhu?

The vendor keeps working.

**MAN #1**
Kaasu.

The vendor doesn't answer.

Kavin arrives carrying two flower baskets.

He sees the tension.

He puts the baskets down.

**KAVIN**
Enna problem?

The vendor looks at him.

Then at the three men.

**VENDOR**
Onnum illa da.

Kavin looks at the men.

**KAVIN**
Appo ivanga yen inga statue maadhiri nikraanga?

The younger vendors suppress a laugh.

The three men don't.

MAN #1 walks toward Kavin.

**MAN #1**
Nee romba pesura.

Kavin smiles.

**KAVIN**
Naan pesina dhaan unakku problem-aa?

The man steps closer.

**MAN #1**
Durai oda paiyan-nu nenachittu romba scene podadha.

Kavin's smile disappears for a second.

**KAVIN**
Paiyan illa.

Beat.

**KAVIN**
Durai anna oda aal.

The man stares at him.

Kavin picks up a flower basket.

Walks away.

---

### EXT. SIDE STREET — MOMENTS LATER

Kavin turns a corner.

He stops.

His expression changes.

He checks behind him.

Nobody.

He reaches into his pocket.

A tiny folded piece of paper.

He opens it.

A handwritten list of names.

Three names are crossed out.

One name remains.

**KAVIN (under his breath)**
Maaran...

He folds the paper.

Puts it away.

His normal smile returns.

He walks back toward the market.

---

### INT. MAARAN'S OFFICE — AFTERNOON

Maaran sits with his men.

A map of Brickfields lies on the table.

Several shops are marked.

**MAARAN**
Moonu naal.

The men listen.

**MAARAN**
Moonu naal-la Brickfields full-aa namma collection varanum.

One man hesitates.

**MAN #2**
Durai irukkaan.

Maaran looks at him.

**MAARAN**
Durai oru aal.

He points at the map.

**MAARAN**
Idhu oru area.

Beat.

**MAARAN**
Area-a bayappadutha mudinja... oru aal thevai illa.

The man nods.

Maaran looks at another man.

**MAARAN**
Rahim?

**MAN #3**
Innum pay panla.

Maaran smiles.

**MAARAN**
Avan kadai first.

---

# SCENE 7 — RAHIM'S LAST WARNING

### INT. RAHIM'S MAMAK SHOP — NIGHT

The shop is nearly empty.

Rahim closes the cash drawer.

He hears the bell above the door.

Two men enter.

The same collectors.

Rahim immediately knows.

**RAHIM**
Anna... naan already sonnen.

**MAN #1**
Naan kekka varala.

He places the envelope on the counter.

**MAN #1**
Kadai key.

Rahim freezes.

**RAHIM**
Enna?

**MAN #1**
Inga irundhu temporary-aa close pannidu.

Rahim stares.

**RAHIM**
Idhu en kadai.

**MAN #1**
Ippo illa.

The second man begins pulling down chairs.

Rahim steps forward.

**RAHIM**
Kai vechadha.

The man laughs.

**MAN #2**
Enna pannuva?

A voice comes from outside.

**KAVIN (O.S.)**
Avan onnum panna maataan.

Everyone turns.

Kavin stands at the doorway.

Holding a small flower basket.

**KAVIN**
Aana naan koopduven.

**MAN #1**
Yaarai?

Kavin looks at him.

**KAVIN**
Durai anna-va.

Silence.

The man laughs.

**MAN #1**
Poi koopdu.

Kavin smiles.

**KAVIN**
Adhaan naanum solren.

He turns and walks out.

The men look at each other.

---

### EXT. RAHIM'S MAMAK SHOP — CONTINUOUS

Kavin walks down the street.

He takes out his phone.

He doesn't call Durai.

Instead, he dials an unknown number.

**KAVIN**
Avanga move pannitaanga.

He listens.

**KAVIN**
Rahim shop.

Pause.

**KAVIN**
Innikki night.

He hangs up.

A motorcycle passes behind him.

Kavin watches it.

His face becomes serious.

**KAVIN**
Seri.

He walks away.

---

### INT. DURAI'S HOUSE — NIGHT

Durai sits quietly.

Kavin enters.

**DURAI**
Rahim?

**KAVIN**
Problem.

Durai looks at him.

Kavin explains.

Durai listens without interrupting.

**DURAI**
Avanukku sollu... kadai close panna vendaam.

**KAVIN**
Avanga force pannuvaanga.

Durai looks at him.

**DURAI**
Appo avanga force pannattum.

Beat.

**DURAI**
Namma paakkalaam.

Kavin nods.

**KAVIN**
Anna...

Durai looks up.

**KAVIN**
Neenga romba calm-aa irukkinga.

Durai gives a small smile.

**DURAI**
Kovam vandha odane kai use panna easy.

Beat.

**DURAI**
Aana kai use pannaama problem solve pannradhu dhaan kashtam.

Kavin smiles.

**KAVIN**
Seri, Durai anna.

---

# SCENE 8 — THE TRAP

### EXT. RAHIM'S MAMAK SHOP — LATE NIGHT

Rain begins.

Rahim locks the front door.

But the lights stay on.

Across the street, a black car waits.

Inside, Maaran's men watch.

One checks his phone.

**MAN**
Shop open.

A voice answers.

**MAARAN (V.O.)**
Wait.

Inside the shop—

Rahim sits alone.

Kavin is at the back, unseen.

Durai stands near the kitchen.

**RAHIM**
Anna... idhu sariyaa?

**DURAI**
Nee bayappadaadha.

**RAHIM**
Aana?

Durai looks at him.

**DURAI**
Unakku kadai venum.

Beat.

**DURAI**
Enakku unakku kadai irukkanum.

Rahim nods.

---

### EXT. BACK LANE — SAME TIME

A van slowly enters the lane.

Two men get out.

They carry sealed boxes.

Kavin watches from a dark doorway.

He recognizes the vehicle.

His expression changes.

He quietly takes a photograph with his phone.

One of the men turns.

Kavin steps back.

The man doesn't see him.

Kavin waits.

The van leaves.

He looks at the photograph.

A registration number.

A location.

A face.

He sends the image to the unknown number.

**KAVIN**
Confirmed.

He puts the phone away.

---

### INT. MAARAN'S CAR — SAME NIGHT

Maaran receives a message.

He looks at the screen.

Nothing.

He calls one of his men.

**MAARAN**
Van enga?

**MAN (V.O.)**
Rahim shop side.

Maaran's eyes narrow.

**MAARAN**
Rahim shop-aa?

Beat.

**MAARAN**
Kavin anga irukkaana?

The man hesitates.

**MAN**
Theriyala.

Maaran sits forward.

**MAARAN**
Theriyala-na therinjiko.

He ends the call.

---

### EXT. RAHIM'S MAMAK SHOP — MOMENTS LATER

A black MPV stops.

Three men get out.

The front door opens.

They enter.

A beat.

Then—

The lights go out.

We hear movement.

A chair falls.

A man groans.

The lights come back on.

Durai stands in the centre.

The three men are on the floor.

Kavin stands beside Rahim.

Durai looks at them.

**DURAI**
Kadai-la vandhu problem panna...

He points to the door.

**DURAI**
Velila po.

One man struggles up.

**MAN**
Maaran anna—

Durai cuts him off.

**DURAI**
Avan peru sollaadha.

Beat.

**DURAI**
Avan varattum.

The men leave.

Kavin looks at Durai.

Something about the night has changed.

---

# SCENE 9 — DURAI AND MAARAN

### EXT. OLD TEMPLE STREET — NIGHT

The rain has stopped.

The street is almost empty.

Durai walks alone.

A black MPV stops.

Maaran steps out.

No men.

Just him.

Durai stops.

They look at each other.

Years of reputation between them.

Maaran smiles.

**MAARAN**
Durai.

**DURAI**
Maaran.

**MAARAN**
Namma first time meet panrom.

**DURAI**
Theriyum.

**MAARAN**
Appo direct-aa pesalaam.

Durai waits.

**MAARAN**
Brickfields-la naan business panna poren.

**DURAI**
Pannu.

Maaran smiles.

**MAARAN**
Neenga problem panna koodadhu.

Durai looks around.

**DURAI**
Nee business panra.

Beat.

**DURAI**
Naan problem panna maaten.

Maaran smiles.

**MAARAN**
Good.

Durai looks directly at him.

**DURAI**
Aana un business-la innocent people-ku problem vandha...

Beat.

**DURAI**
Appo naan business-a paakka maaten.

Maaran's smile fades.

**MAARAN**
Neenga enna pannuveenga?

Durai steps closer.

**DURAI**
Unakku theriyum.

Maaran laughs quietly.

**MAARAN**
Neenga old school.

**DURAI**
Nee new school.

Beat.

**DURAI**
Aana manushan bayappadra reason rendu school-layum same dhaan.

Maaran stares.

Durai walks past him.

Maaran turns.

**MAARAN**
Durai!

Durai stops.

**MAARAN**
Kavin-a pathi konjam careful-aa irunga.

Durai slowly turns.

**MAARAN**
Young blood.

Beat.

**MAARAN**
Young blood-ku romba seekiram kovam varum.

Durai looks at him.

**DURAI**
Kavin pathi pesadha.

Maaran smiles.

**MAARAN**
Why?

Durai's eyes harden.

**DURAI**
Avan enakku business illa.

Beat.

**DURAI**
Avan enakku family.

Maaran nods slowly.

**MAARAN**
Appo purinjiduchu.

He gets into the car.

The MPV leaves.

Durai remains standing.

---

# SCENE 10 — KAVIN DISAPPEARS

### INT. DURAI'S GARLAND SHOP — NEXT MORNING

Kavin is missing.

The shop is open.

Flowers are ready.

But Kavin isn't there.

Durai checks his phone.

No message.

Rahim enters.

**RAHIM**
Kavin enga?

Durai shakes his head.

**RAHIM**
Call pannala?

**DURAI**
Illa.

Rahim looks worried.

**RAHIM**
Maaran side?

Durai doesn't answer.

---

### EXT. BACK STREET — DAY

Kavin's motorcycle lies on its side.

A flower basket is broken beside it.

Durai arrives.

He looks at the scene.

He kneels.

Picks up a crushed jasmine garland.

His face remains calm.

Too calm.

Rahim stands behind him.

**RAHIM**
Anna...

Durai stands.

**DURAI**
Kavin-a kandupidikanum.

---

### INT. ABANDONED STORAGE ROOM — DAY

Kavin sits on a chair.

His hands are tied.

Maaran stands in front of him.

Two men behind Kavin.

**MAARAN**
Nee romba brave.

Kavin looks up.

**KAVIN**
Thanks.

Maaran smiles.

**MAARAN**
Joke pannadha.

**KAVIN**
Neenga dhaane kooptinga.

Maaran walks around him.

**MAARAN**
Durai-ku nee romba mukkiyam.

Kavin says nothing.

Maaran leans closer.

**MAARAN**
Unakku theriyuma?

Kavin looks at him.

**MAARAN**
Durai weak point enna-nu?

Kavin smiles faintly.

**KAVIN**
Theriyum.

Maaran waits.

**KAVIN**
Avan romba nallavan.

Maaran laughs.

**MAARAN**
Adhu weak point-aa?

Kavin's eyes remain steady.

**KAVIN**
Aama.

Beat.

**KAVIN**
Aana adha use panna mudiyuma-nu theriyala.

Maaran's expression changes.

He punches Kavin.

Kavin takes it.

Doesn't react.

Maaran walks away.

**MAARAN**
Durai-ku call pannunga.

One man takes out a phone.

---

### INT. DURAI'S GARLAND SHOP — SAME TIME

The phone rings.

Durai answers.

**MAARAN (V.O.)**
Durai.

Durai says nothing.

**MAARAN**
Kavin en kitta irukkaan.

Silence.

Durai's face changes.

**MAARAN**
Brickfields-a vittu veliya po.

Beat.

**MAARAN**
Illana Kavin—

Durai cuts him off.

**DURAI**
Kavin-a onnum pannaadha.

Maaran smiles.

**MAARAN**
Appo?

Durai looks toward the empty chair where Kavin usually sits.

**DURAI**
Naan varen.

He hangs up.

Rahim looks at him.

**RAHIM**
Anna...

Durai picks up his keys.

**DURAI**
Kavin-a veetukku kondu varanum.

---

# SCENE 11 — THE TRUTH BEGINS

### INT. ABANDONED STORAGE ROOM — NIGHT

The room is dim.

Maaran sits opposite Kavin.

**MAARAN**
Durai varuvaan.

Kavin says nothing.

Maaran puts a phone on the table.

**MAARAN**
Nee romba loyal.

Kavin looks at him.

**MAARAN**
Durai-ku.

Kavin smiles.

**KAVIN**
Adha doubt panna vendam.

Maaran leans back.

**MAARAN**
Unakku oru chance.

Beat.

**MAARAN**
Durai enga?

Kavin laughs.

**KAVIN**
Enna?

**MAARAN**
Avan enna plan panraan?

Kavin looks at him.

**KAVIN**
Neenga romba kelvi kekkuringa.

Maaran slams the table.

**MAARAN**
Sollu!

Kavin's expression suddenly becomes cold.

For the first time, the harmless flower boy disappears.

**KAVIN**
Nee dhaan sollanum.

Maaran pauses.

Kavin continues.

**KAVIN**
Un godown-la irundha blue van...

Maaran's face changes.

**KAVIN**
Thursday night...

Beat.

**KAVIN**
Port Klang route...

Maaran slowly stands.

**MAARAN**
Unakku eppadi theriyum?

Kavin smiles.

**KAVIN**
Naan flower sellra paiyan dhaane?

Maaran stares.

A long beat.

Then he punches Kavin again.

This time Kavin catches his wrist.

Everyone freezes.

Kavin releases him.

Returns to the harmless posture.

**KAVIN**
Kovapadadhe.

Maaran looks at him carefully.

Something is wrong.

Very wrong.

---

# SCENE 12 — THE NIGHT OF THE FALL

### EXT. ABANDONED GODOWN — NIGHT

Rain begins again.

Durai's car arrives.

He gets out alone.

No weapons.

No men.

He walks toward the entrance.

Two guards step forward.

Durai looks at them.

**DURAI**
Kavin.

One guard points inside.

Durai enters.

---

### INT. ABANDONED GODOWN — CONTINUOUS

Long rows of boxes.

Maaran stands at the centre.

Kavin is seated on a chair.

His hands appear tied.

Durai sees him.

**DURAI**
Kavin.

Kavin looks up.

**KAVIN**
Anna...

Durai looks at Maaran.

**DURAI**
Avana vidu.

Maaran laughs.

**MAARAN**
Neenga command panreenga.

**DURAI**
Aama.

Maaran steps closer.

**MAARAN**
Inga command naan.

Durai doesn't move.

**MAARAN**
Brickfields-la naan.

**DURAI**
Nee mistake panra.

**MAARAN**
Enna?

Durai looks around.

**DURAI**
Nee Brickfields-a vangala.

Beat.

**DURAI**
Nee konjam bayatha vaangirukka.

Maaran's expression hardens.

He punches Durai.

Durai stumbles.

Kavin's face changes.

Maaran punches him again.

Durai falls to one knee.

Kavin stands.

The ropes fall away.

Maaran turns.

**MAARAN**
Enna?

Kavin looks at the ropes.

Then at Maaran.

**KAVIN**
Idhu?

He smiles.

**KAVIN**
Just show.

One of Maaran's men charges.

Kavin moves.

A fast strike.

The man hits the floor.

Another comes.

Kavin blocks.

Throws him into a table.

Silence.

Durai looks at Kavin.

Not surprised.

Just watching.

Maaran stares.

**MAARAN**
Nee...

Kavin walks toward him.

**MAARAN**
Nee fight panna theriyuma?

Kavin stops.

**KAVIN**
Theriyum.

Beat.

**KAVIN**
Naan romba naal sollala.

Maaran attacks.

Kavin dodges.

Maaran swings again.

Kavin blocks.

The fight is brutal and close.

Maaran is stronger than expected.

Kavin is faster.

Finally Kavin takes him down.

Maaran hits the floor.

Kavin stands over him.

Durai watches.

**DURAI**
Kavin.

Kavin stops.

Durai looks at him.

**DURAI**
Podhum.

Kavin steps back.

Maaran struggles to breathe.

He looks at Kavin.

**MAARAN**
Yaaru nee?

Kavin looks at him.

No smile now.

**KAVIN**
Six months.

Maaran stares.

**KAVIN**
Un kooda.

Beat.

**KAVIN**
Un aalunga kooda.

Maaran's face slowly changes.

Memories flash.

Kavin carrying boxes.

Kavin sitting in the godown.

Kavin laughing with Maaran's men.

Kavin riding deliveries.

Every harmless moment.

Every time Maaran ignored him.

Kavin continues.

**KAVIN**
Nee nenaicha maadhiri naan Durai oda flower paiyan illa.

Maaran breathes heavily.

**KAVIN**
Un organisation-kulla naan six months irundhen.

Beat.

**KAVIN**
Un porul'eh enga pogudhu...

**KAVIN**
Yaaru handle panraanga...

**KAVIN**
Yaaru yaarukku report panraanga...

He looks at Maaran.

**KAVIN**
Ellam theriyum.

Maaran laughs weakly.

**MAARAN**
Police-aa?

Kavin shakes his head.

**KAVIN**
Police illa.

Durai looks at Kavin.

This is the first time the audience sees that Durai knew something.

Maaran notices.

**MAARAN**
Durai-ku theriyuma?

Kavin looks at Durai.

Durai says nothing.

Kavin turns back.

**KAVIN**
Enna panna pora-nu theriyum.

Maaran laughs again.

Then coughs.

**MAARAN**
Nee enna nenachittu irukka?

Beat.

**MAARAN**
Idhoda mudinjiduchu-nu?

Kavin says nothing.

Maaran reaches for his phone.

Kavin kicks it away.

Maaran looks at the phone.

Then at Kavin.

A smile slowly appears.

**MAARAN**
Enna vida periya problem irukku unakku.

Durai steps closer.

**DURAI**
Yaaru?

Maaran doesn't answer.

He reaches for another phone hidden inside his shirt.

Kavin tries to stop him.

Too late.

Maaran calls.

The phone rings.

Once.

Twice.

A man answers.

We never hear his voice clearly.

Maaran speaks with difficulty.

**MAARAN**
Boss...

Silence.

Maaran looks at Durai.

Then Kavin.

**MAARAN**
Brickfields matter...

Beat.

**MAARAN**
Kavin...

He struggles to breathe.

**MAARAN**
Kavin inside vandhuttaan.

Silence on the phone.

Maaran listens.

His face slowly changes.

Fear.

Real fear.

He looks toward Durai.

**MAARAN**
Boss...

Beat.

**MAARAN**
Naan handle panna mudiyala.

A long silence.

The unseen man says something.

We do not hear it.

Maaran's eyes widen.

The call ends.

Kavin looks at Durai.

Durai looks at Maaran.

Nobody speaks.
