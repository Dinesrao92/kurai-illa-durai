# 🎬 KURAI ILLA, DURAI
### A Malaysian Tamil Gangster Mini-Drama (Thanglish)
**Setting:** Late night. A dark back alley behind Jalan Tun Sambanthan, Brickfields, KL. Rain just stopped. Durai Anna stands under a lamppost. Kavin kneels in front of him.

**Characters:**
- **DURAI ANNA** — 50s, old-school Brickfields gang boss. Calm but deadly.
- **KAVIN** — 20s, Durai's loyal young follower ("thambi").
- **MAARAN** — 30s, cocky rival gangster taking over KL streets.

---

## SCENE 1 — THE INTERROGATION

*(SFX: rain dripping, distant KL monorail passing)*

**DURAI:**
> Dey Kavin! Enakku theriyaadhu-nu nenachiya? Brickfields-la nadakkara ella vishayamum en kaadhukku varum da. Pathu varushama indha area yaaru kayila irukku-nu marandhutiya? Sollu da... yaaru andha thurogi?

**KAVIN:** *(panicking)*
> Anne, naan unmaiya solren anne! Naan onnum pannala. Maaran dhaan ellathayum thiruppi pottutaan. Ennai nambunga anne, please! En kudumbatha nenachavadhu oru chance kudunga anne!

**DURAI:** *(cold)*
> Chance-a? Dey, chance ellam cinema-la dhaan da. Naan unakku saapadu potten, velai kudutthen, un thangachi kalyanathukku kaasu kudutthen. Aana nee enna pannita? En lorry-la vandha saman engey da? Yaarukitta kudutha?

## SCENE 2 — THE RIVAL ARRIVES

*(SFX: slow footsteps, slow clap echoing in the alley)*

**MAARAN:** *(smirking, walking in)*
> Oh Durai! Innum pazhaya raja maadhiri pesariya? Kaalam maariduchu da. Ippa indha KL theru ellam en kattupaattula dhaan irukku. Un aatkale ippa en pakkam da. Enna pannuva ippa?

**DURAI:** *(unshaken)*
> Maaran... nee inga varuva-nu enakku theriyum da. Aana oru vishayam marandhutta. Indha Durai veezhndhadhe illa da. Pathu varushama ethana peru vandhaanga, ethana peru poitaanga. Nee enna periya aalu?

**MAARAN:** *(laughing, pointing at Kavin)*
> Haha! Periya aalu dhaan da. Yen theriyuma? Un veettukulla irukkara paambu... un sondha thambi maadhiri valartha indha Kavin dhaan da enakku ella thagaval kuduthadhu! Correct dhaana, Kavin?

## SCENE 3 — THE TWIST 🔥

*(Kavin slowly stands up... and smiles)*

**KAVIN:** *(calm now, totally different tone)*
> Sorry Maaran... naan un aalu illa da. Durai anna sonnadhala dhaan un kooda sendhen. Indha aaru maasam nee pannadhu ellam... police kitta proof-a irukku da. Veliya paaru... sirens kekudha?

*(SFX: police sirens approaching, blue-red lights flashing on the walls)*

**MAARAN:** *(shocked, grabbed by unseen officers)*
> Enna?! Dey Kavin! Nee... nee ennai yemathittiya?! Durai! Idhu nyaayama da? Vidu da ennai! Naan unnai summa vida maatten!

**DURAI:** *(lighting a cigarette, calm)*
> Maaran... Brickfields-la oru pazhamozhi irukku da. Puli pasichaalum pullai thinnadhu. Aana nee? Sondha aatkalaye vithu putta. Kavin... police kitta kootitu po da ivanai.

## FINALE

*(Sirens fade. Durai puts his arm around Kavin's shoulder)*

**KAVIN:** *(grinning)*
> Anne... adutha vaati indha maadhiri acting velai kudukkadheenga anne. Mutti la ukkandhu ukkandhu kaal valikkudhu! Aana anna... namma plan vera level ah work out aaduchu la?

*(Both laugh. Camera pulls back over the KL skyline, Petronas Towers glowing)*

**DURAI:** *(final line, looking at the city)*
> Dey thambi... indha KL-la raja maaralam, aana Brickfields-la oru vishayam maaraadhu da. Indha area... eppovume Durai area dhaan! Vaa da, mamak kadaila roti canai saapidalam. Naan belanja!

*(SFX: theme music swells — END)*
