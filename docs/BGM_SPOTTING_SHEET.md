# 🎼 KURAI ILLA, DURAI — CHAPTER 1
## STORY SUMMARY + BGM SPOTTING SHEET
*For the composer-director: one theme per character, one mood per scene.*

---

# 📖 THE STORY IN ONE BREATH

Brickfields has a king nobody calls a king. **Durai** — late 50s, garland seller by day, legend by reputation — protects his streets with fear, but never profits from it. Ten years ago he pulled an orphan boy off the night street; today that boy, **Kavin**, is his left hand in the flower trade — sweet, playful, "cannot even fight." From Johor comes **Maaran**, a real criminal — drugs, smuggling, murder — who thinks the old man is a relic to be replaced. He squeezes the kedais, ambushes the flower lorry, baits a trap at Rahim's mamak shop. Durai bends but never breaks his code. Then Kavin disappears — "kidnapped" by Maaran's men. In the godown climax, Maaran attacks Durai... and the harmless boy walks in and DISMANTLES everyone. The reveal: Kavin spent **six months undercover inside Maaran's own organisation**, protecting his anna from the inside. Maaran falls — but with his last strength he calls someone ABOVE him. A face we never see. Chapter 1 ends: the king keeps his streets, dawn returns, jasmine again... but a bigger shadow is already dialing.

**CORE THEME: Maaran uses fear to control people. Durai uses fear to protect them.**

---

# 🎵 THE FOUR THEMES (compose these first, reuse everywhere)

| Theme | Character | Sound identity | Instruments |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌼 **JASMINE THEME** | Durai | Calm, old-soul dignity hiding thunder. Slow, sparse, never hurried — like the man | Solo veena or nylon guitar + deep tanpura drone + soft strings. When danger: add ONE urumi hit under it |
| 🪁 **THAMBI THEME** | Kavin | Light, cheeky, street-boy warmth — with a hidden minor-key tail nobody notices until the climax | Flute/whistle + kanjira + acoustic strum. Climax version: SAME melody, half-speed, heavy percussion + brass |
| 🐍 **JOHOR THEME** | Maaran | Oily menace, money and muscle. Modern, synthetic — he doesn't belong to these streets | Sub-bass pulse + industrial percussion + distorted cello stabs |
| 📱 **SHADOW MOTIF** | Mysterious Man | 3–4 notes MAX. Cold, empty, unresolved. Never develops — because we never see him | Single piano high notes over silence / reversed pad. Must feel UNFINISHED |

**Golden rule:** Durai's theme = acoustic/organic. Maaran's = electronic/synthetic. The war of sounds IS the war of worlds. When Kavin's reveal comes, his flute theme transforms into war percussion — same notes, new monster.

# 🎬 SCENE-BY-SCENE SPOTTING

## SC 1 — BRICKFIELDS BEFORE DAWN (5:15 AM)
**Story:** Pre-dawn. Hands thread jasmine. The city wakes around a quiet man everyone greets with respect. "Naan Durai." We meet the king disguised as a flower seller.
**Mood:** Sacred stillness → quiet authority
**BGM:** 🌼 Jasmine Theme, fullest and purest version. Tanpura drone + temple bell distant + veena. NO percussion until his name drops — then one deep thavil hit. ~60 BPM.

## SC 2 — TEN YEARS EARLIER (flashback)
**Story:** Rain, night. Durai finds the orphan boy. Food, shelter, no speeches. Montage: ten years — boy becomes Kavin, garlands, teaching, laughter. Back to present.
**Mood:** Melancholy → warmth → family
**BGM:** Start Jasmine Theme stripped to single instrument + rain. As the montage grows, 🪁 Thambi Theme is BORN — flute enters shyly, then both themes play TOGETHER in harmony (this duet = the anna-thambi sound; you'll destroy hearts replaying it in Sc12). ~70 BPM swelling.

## SC 3 — MAARAN'S WORLD
**Story:** Abandoned godown, Johor crew. Maaran holds court — casual cruelty, big plans for Brickfields. Next morning: his "gift" package arrives at Durai's shop. The smile that isn't a smile.
**Mood:** Menace, invasion
**BGM:** 🐍 Johor Theme debut — sub-bass heartbeat, metallic clanks (godown = percussion instrument!). When the package reaches the shop: Johor Theme leaks INTO Jasmine Theme's space — first time the two sounds touch. Dissonance, no resolution. ~90 BPM.

## SC 4 — THE FIRST MOVE
**Story:** Maaran's men walk Brickfields like they own it. Rahim's mamak pressured. Durai answers quietly — the envelope, "Monthly" — protection the old way. Maaran watches from a car in the dark.
**Mood:** Chess opening, tension under politeness
**BGM:** Alternate themes like a conversation: Johor pulse when the thugs walk / Jasmine calm when Durai responds. Keep drums MINIMAL — this is cold war, not war. Tabla/kanjira light ticking like a clock. ~80 BPM.

## SC 5 — THE BOUNDARY
**Story:** Flower market morning. Then night: the lorry ambush — baskets overturned, garlands in the mud, rain. Durai sees the ruined flowers. He doesn't rage. He goes STILL. "Manushan-a illa..." The still man is scarier than the loud one. Maaran's office celebrates too early.
**Mood:** Beauty destroyed → terrifying stillness
**BGM:** THE centerpiece. Garland fall = strings suspended, time stops, almost silence — let rain be the percussion. When Durai goes still: DRONE ONLY + one slow urumi scrape (the sound of a sleeping lion's eye opening). NO melody — stillness has no tune. End with first-ever LOW brass note under Jasmine Theme: the king's war face. ~50 BPM or free time.

## SC 6 — THE PRICE OF FEAR
**Story:** Market traders squeezed, fear spreading. Side street: thugs test Durai directly — wrist catch, bored face. Maaran learns the old man doesn't scare.
**Mood:** Pressure + first physical spark
**BGM:** Johor pulse grows louder in the market (fear spreading = his theme spreading). The wrist catch: SILENCE → one massive thavil+urumi DOUBLE HIT on the catch → silence again. Durai doesn't need a fight cue; he needs ONE hit. ~85 BPM.

## SC 7 — RAHIM'S LAST WARNING
**Story:** Night at the mamak. Rahim, scared, warns Durai: this one is different, bigger, connected. Durai at home — quiet, heavy. The weight of protecting everyone.
**Mood:** Dread, intimacy, loneliness of the protector
**BGM:** Saddest Jasmine variation — solo veena, slow, few notes, long silences. Maybe first subtle 📱 Shadow Motif piano notes VERY far away (Rahim says "bigger" — the audience's spine should notice before their brain). ~55 BPM.

## SC 8 — THE TRAP
**Story:** Late night ambush staged at Rahim's shop. Back lane: a hand grabs Kavin's shoulder — he calmly pries it off, eyes unbothered... (first crack in the "harmless boy" mask, but audience doesn't clock it yet). Maaran watches from the car.
**Mood:** Night tension, something off about the boy
**BGM:** Johor Theme driving the trap. BUT — during Kavin's pry-off: slip THREE notes of Thambi Theme in a minor key, low in the mix. Subliminal. On rewatch, fans will scream. End on light-out = music cuts DEAD with the bulb. ~100 BPM.

## SC 9 — DURAI AND MAARAN (face to face)
**Story:** Old temple street, night. The two kings finally talk. Durai gives him one chance to walk away. Maaran laughs. Later: Maaran's office — the glass shatters, the phone slams. "Durai-a touch panna sollala naan... Brickfields-a touch panna sonnen." War declared.
**Mood:** Titans measuring each other → villain eruption
**BGM:** Both full themes in the SAME cue, taking turns, neither winning — veena vs sub-bass, trading bars. For Maaran's rage: Johor Theme at its ugliest — distortion, broken rhythm, glass-shard metallics. ~75 then 110 BPM.

## SC 10 — KAVIN DISAPPEARS
**Story:** Morning — Kavin is gone. Taken. Storage room: the boy tied to a chair, thugs circling. The shop without him is silent. Durai hears — and at the end of this scene, the humble white shirt comes off. The boss returns.
**Mood:** Panic → grief → TRANSFORMATION
**BGM:** Thambi Theme broken — flute plays WRONG, incomplete phrases (the missing boy = missing notes). For the costume change: Jasmine Theme reborn as a WAR MARCH — full thavil battery, urumi, brass. The scene everyone will replay. Start ~60, end ~120 BPM.

## SC 11 — THE TRUTH BEGINS
**Story:** Night, storage room. Tied Kavin and his captors — but the conversations feel wrong. Kavin is too calm. Threads start showing: who is playing whom?
**Mood:** Unease, puzzle pieces moving
**BGM:** Minor-key Thambi Theme now OPENLY leading the cue for the first time — audience permission to suspect. Ticking percussion (clock motif). Sprinkle Shadow Motif once. ~90 BPM.

## SC 12 — THE NIGHT OF THE FALL (climax)
**Story:** Godown. Durai vs Maaran — verbal fire, then Maaran attacks. And then... the ropes fall. Kavin stands up FREE, smiling — and the harmless thambi destroys Maaran's entire crew, overwhelms Maaran, stands over him. REVEAL: six months undercover inside Maaran's organisation. For his anna. Broken Maaran, bleeding on the floor, makes one phone call... to a man whose face we never see.
**Mood:** Explosion → jaw-drop → triumph → ICE
**BGM:** The whole movie's payoff in 4 moves:
1. Confrontation: both themes colliding at full power, ~130 BPM
2. **ROPES FALL = FULL SILENCE.** 2 seconds. Nothing. Let the smile land.
3. Kavin fights: 🪁 Thambi Theme TRANSFORMED — same childhood flute melody, now on brass + full urumi/parai war drums. The lullaby became a weapon. This cue sells the entire twist.
4. The phone call: everything dies to 📱 Shadow Motif — 3 cold piano notes + room tone. Unresolved. Hold it. CUT.

## FINAL IMAGE — PRE-DAWN AGAIN
**Story:** Brickfields, 5 AM, jasmine again. The circle closes — Durai threading garlands, Kavin beside him. Peace... then: unknown room, a man we can't see, a decision being made. CUT TO BLACK. "KURAI ILLA, DURAI."
**Mood:** Earned peace with a poisoned edge
**BGM:** Jasmine + Thambi duet one last time, warmest version — then as we cut to the unknown room, the duet DETUNES under Shadow Motif. End title: one final massive thavil hit → silence. ~60 BPM.

# 🎚️ COMPOSER'S CHEAT CARD

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Durai = acoustic, Maaran = electronic | The war of worlds is a war of sounds |
| Durai's power = SILENCE + one hit | Still man scarier than loud man |
| Hide minor-key Kavin notes in Sc2/8/11 | Plants the twist — pays off huge on rewatch |
| Ropes fall = 2s total silence | The biggest hit in the film is no sound at all |
| Shadow Motif never resolves | Unfinished music = unfinished story = Part 2 |
| Rain is percussion | Sc2, Sc5, Sc8 — don't fight it, score WITH it |
