# KURAI ILLA, DURAI — CHAPTER 1 TRAILER
### EXPANDED 60 SECONDS · 9:16 · 720p · Kling 2.5 Turbo
### Direction: DR

## DIRECTOR'S INTENT

This replaces the 15-second teaser as the main trailer concept. It must feel like a theatrical crime-drama trailer, not a montage of pretty AI shots.

Sell: Durai's code and bond with Brickfields; Kavin's harmless image; Maaran's arrival; the flower-market attack; Durai's controlled authority; a mysterious glimpse of Kavin's hidden capability; the existence of a larger power above Maaran; and a final collision that makes viewers want Chapter 1.

DO NOT reveal Kavin's six-month undercover operation. DO NOT show police. DO NOT reveal the mysterious man's face. Do not make Durai a flashy action hero. His power comes from stillness, timing and control.

## SHOT 01 — BRICKFIELDS BEFORE DAWN
Reference: refs/brickfields_street.jpg or strongest Brickfields dawn reference.

Start locked-off at street level. Wet asphalt foreground. A flower lorry enters deep background. Camera executes a slow controlled 5-metre backward dolly as it approaches. A flower basket passes very close to lens as a natural wipe, revealing jasmine stalls behind it. Photorealistic Malaysian Indian flower market, realistic rain residue, sodium dawn light, restrained theatrical colour, no text, no logos, no handheld shake.

Audio: lorry engine, reverse beeper, birds, metal baskets, distant mamak ambience.

## SHOT 02 — THE HANDS
Reference: scenes_v4/s1_A_hands_jasmine_dawn.png.

Extreme macro on Durai's weathered hands. Slow 20 cm push-in. At 2 seconds, tiny clockwise arc around his fingers. Jasmine thread pulls tight. He cuts the thread with his teeth. Hard cut before full face reveal.

VO: "Naan Durai."

## SHOT 03 — DURAI REVEAL
Reference: posters/durai_reveal_poster.png.

Durai sits among flowers. Camera begins behind shoulder, then slowly tracks laterally to reveal his profile. He checks the garland and only at the final second raises his eyes toward camera. Play him as a respected neighbourhood man carrying dangerous authority, not a flashy gangster.

VO: "Brickfields-la poo vikkiren..."

## SHOT 04 — KAVIN
Reference: part1_v3/intro_shot_kavin_v2.png.

Kavin hangs a garland, fixes one loose flower, hears someone call him, turns with a casual smile and walks through the stall. Gentle lateral tracking. Natural Malaysian Indian humour.

Kavin: "Enna, anna-ku attendance poda sollriya?"

Make the audience like him.

## SHOT 05 — TEN YEARS
Three rapid inserts: young Kavin soaked and barefoot under a shop awning; Durai teaching him jasmine and Kavin dropping the basket; adult Kavin riding beside Durai through Brickfields. Match cut from young Kavin's hand holding jasmine to adult Kavin holding a finished garland.

VO: "Pathu varusham..."

Understated emotion, not melodrama.

## SHOT 06 — JOHOR ARRIVES
Reference: part1_v3/intro_shot_maaran_v2.png.

Black MPV stops. Low camera near front wheel. Wheel rolls to stop close to lens. Door opens. Maaran's shoe lands. Camera cranes from shoe to torso. He adjusts his cuff. Bodyguards emerge behind him. Maaran walks forward without looking back.

Optional Maaran line: "Brickfields-la ippo yaaru side-nu decide pannradhu naan."

Audio: wet tyres, door slam, three low impact hits.

## SHOT 07 — THE PACKAGE
Reference: scenes_v3/insert_C_kavin_phone_tease.png or strongest crime insert.

Close-up of a plastic packet placed on a wooden table beside a flower garland. Camera tracks along table edge. Rack focus from illegal goods to jasmine. Cut before explaining the contents.

Maaran VO: "Idhu Brickfields poganum."

## SHOT 08 — THE LINE
Recreate Scene 1. Two-shot across Durai's flower stall. Durai remains seated while Maaran's messenger delivers the threat. Slow dolly toward Durai. At "Idhu Brickfields" push slowly into his eyes.

Messenger: "Maaran anna solli anuppichaaru."
Durai: "Business-ku problem illa."
Durai: "Aana... unga business enna-nu therinjikanum."
Durai: "Adha inga kondu vara koodadhu."
Durai: "Naan Maaran illa. Idhu Brickfields."

No shouting. The silence after the line is the threat.

## SHOT 09 — RAHIM'S SHOP
Reference: keep/ref_rahim.png.

Envelope of cash lands on mamak table. Rahim looks toward the door. Kavin appears deep background carrying flowers. Rack focus from money to Kavin. Kavin notices but says nothing.

Maaran's man: "Monthly."
Rahim: "Kudukkala-na?"
Man: "Problem varum."

## SHOT 10 — FLOWER LORRY ATTACK
Reference: scenes_v4/s5_F_flowers_in_mud.png.

First major action sequence. Flower lorry travels on wet road. Black MPV cuts across. Lorry emergency-brakes. Low camera swings with braking motion. Men pull the driver away from cab. Rear doors slam open. Flower baskets are thrown onto road. Jasmine garland lands in muddy water. Camera drops to flower level as petals scatter.

Driver: "Anna... idhu Durai anna oda load."
Man: "Theriyum."

Camera grammar: controlled vehicle tracking → whip pan on brake → low-angle impact → macro flower insert. Every action must have physical cause and reaction.

## SHOT 11 — THE STILL MAN
Reference: s10_B_crushed_garland.png.

Driver stands soaked before Durai. Kavin is furious. Start on ruined flowers, slowly tilt up to Durai. No push-in until the final line.

Kavin: "Anna... idhu summa vida mudiyuma?"
Durai: "Flowers-a adichirukkaanga."
Beat.
Durai: "Manushan-a illa."
Durai: "Adhukku manushan mela kai vaikka koodadhu."

This is the heart of the trailer.

## SHOT 12 — DURAI MOVES
Night, narrow Brickfields lane. Three Maaran men block Durai. One reaches for Durai's shirt. Durai catches the wrist before contact, rotates it just enough to stop the man, releases him and walks past.

Camera starts behind the three men, 35mm, slow dolly backward. Snap to clean close-up on wrist catch. Return to wide as Durai walks past.

No superhero fighting. One precise movement is more frightening than ten punches.

## SHOT 13 — KAVIN'S SECRET
Dark warehouse. Kavin stands with back to camera. Two silhouettes approach. One grabs his shoulder. Kavin's hand suddenly closes around the man's wrist. He turns his head slightly. Only one eye is visible. CUT TO BLACK.

Do not show the full fight, police, undercover clues or six-month explanation.

Optional Kavin whisper: "Anna sonna... naan kekka maaten-nu ninaichiya?"

The audience should think: "Wait... who the hell is Kavin?"

## SHOT 14 — MAARAN LOSES CONTROL
Reference: refs/card_maaran_v2.png.

Maaran throws a phone onto the table. A glass falls and shatters. For the first time he is genuinely angry. Locked medium shot with broken glass foreground.

Maaran: "Durai-a touch panna sollala naan."
Beat.
Maaran: "Brickfields-a touch panna sonnen."

## SHOT 15 — THE BIGGER SHADOW
Maaran is badly injured in darkness after the climax. He makes a phone call. Show only his hand and phone. Never show the other man's face.

Voice: "Maaran."
Maaran looks frightened.
Mysterious man: "Nee sonna maadhiri... idhu mudinjiduchaa?"

CUT TO BLACK.

This plants Part 2 without revealing the identity.

## SHOT 16 — FINAL COLLISION
Rapid 0.35–0.5 second cuts, each with clear cause and effect:
1. Durai turns sharply.
2. Maaran charges.
3. Kavin steps into frame.
4. Durai catches a punch.
5. Kavin's eyes harden.
6. Maaran falls backward.
7. Jasmine garland hits wet ground.

No random AI fighting. Every movement must connect: step → attack → block → counter → impact → reaction.

## SHOT 17 — TITLE SMASH
Black screen. A single jasmine flower falls. Before it touches ground, KURAI ILLA slams on screen, then DURAI larger. Match final_cast_poster_v3.png. Small line: CHAPTER 1. Final Durai VO: "Kurai illa... Durai." Cut to black.

## KLING 2.5 TURBO PRODUCTION RULES
- Generate each major action as a separate 5-second shot.
- Use portrait references so final edit remains 9:16.
- Kling 2.5 Turbo image-to-video does not expose a native aspect-ratio parameter; framing comes from the reference image.
- Output 720p, native audio ON.
- Generate action beats independently rather than asking one generation to perform several unrelated actions.
- Preserve character reference and wardrobe wherever possible.
- No text inside Kling generations; add titles in edit.
- No police imagery.
- Violence dramatic but non-gory.
- Use real camera language: dolly, lateral track, crane, arc, rack focus, whip pan, locked-off, low-angle tracking.

## FINAL DIRECTOR'S NOTE
This is not a hero introduction. It is the first 60 seconds of a world.

Durai is dangerous because he does not need to prove it.
Maaran is dangerous because everyone around him is afraid.
Kavin is harmless because the audience needs to trust him.
The final Kavin wrist reveal is the rewatch hook.

The audience should leave with three questions:
1. Who is Durai really?
2. What is Kavin hiding?
3. Who is powerful enough to make Maaran afraid?
