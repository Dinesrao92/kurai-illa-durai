## STORY CANON — LOCKED

### DURAI
- Late 50s. Old-school, respected gangster figure in Brickfields.
- Publicly operates multiple legitimate businesses around Brickfields.
- His most visible day-to-day presence is the flower and garland trade.
- Durai does **not** sell drugs, smuggle illegal goods, exploit innocent people, or kill for business.
- He protects the people and neighbourhood around him, but handles threats using the methods and reputation of a gangster.
- His power comes from relationships, loyalty, influence and fear of crossing his code.
- His central moral contrast with Maaran: **Durai is a gangster by reputation and method, but a protector by principle.**

### KAVIN
- Late 20s.
- An orphan whom Durai found on the streets **10 years ago** and raised.
- Durai is effectively Kavin's father figure.
- Kavin is Durai's trusted left hand.
- Kavin specifically handles the **garland/flower business**; he does not run all of Durai's businesses.
- He appears harmless, casual and inexperienced in fighting.
- Unknown to everyone, Kavin spends **six months undercover inside Maaran's organisation**.
- His undercover mission is to expose and trap Maaran's criminal network.
- The audience should not know about the six-month undercover operation until the climax.
- Kavin always addresses Durai as **"Durai Anna"** or **"Anna"**. He never calls him "Appa".

### MAARAN
- The apparent main villain of Part 1.
- A powerful crime boss from **Johor** with a large gang network extending into Kuala Lumpur.
- Involved in drugs, smuggling, murder and other serious criminal activity.
- Uses fear, money and violence to control people.
- He is expanding his operation from Johor into Brickfields.
- He believes Durai is an outdated gangster whose influence can be taken over.
- **Important Part 1 twist:** Maaran is not the true top of the criminal organisation.

### THE MYSTERIOUS MAN
- Maaran's real superior.
- His identity is **not revealed in Part 1**.
- The audience discovers only at the climax that Maaran answers to someone above him.
- This character is the primary bridge into Part 2.

# SCENE 1 — BRICKFIELDS BEFORE DAWN

Before the city wakes up.

## CLIMAX CANON — LOCKED

There is **no police presence in the climax**.

Durai and Maaran have a heated verbal confrontation.

Maaran attacks Durai.

Kavin enters and unexpectedly reveals that he can fight.

The audience and characters have believed Kavin is harmless and cannot fight.

Kavin overwhelms Maaran and badly injures him.

The reveal follows: Kavin has secretly been undercover inside Maaran's organisation for **six months**.

Maaran is not the ultimate villain.

While badly injured, Maaran makes a phone call to a mysterious man who is actually the much bigger head of the crime organisation.

**Part 1 ends without revealing the mysterious man's identity.**

---

## CORE THEME

**Maaran uses fear to control people.  
Durai uses fear to protect them.**

Durai's goodness must be shown through his actions rather than repeatedly explained by other characters.

Kavin's climax reveal is also the payoff to the **10-year bond between Durai and Kavin**: the orphan Durai found on the streets has grown into the man Durai trusted to protect him and the people around them.
